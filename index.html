Popular Abbreviations & Text Slang Meanings

Social & Video

POV — Point of View

Meaning: Originally a film and literature term, "POV" on TikTok and Instagram refers to videos shot from the creator's first-person perspective, placing the viewer directly in a hypothetical scenario.

Example: "POV: You forgot your AirPods on a 6-hour flight."

Pop Culture & Sports

GOAT — Greatest of All Time

Meaning: Used to praise athletes, artists, or innovators who have achieved unrivaled excellence in their field (often accompanied by the 🐐 goat emoji).

Example: "That performance was incredible, he is truly the GOAT."

Texting & Chat

FR / NO CAP — For Real / No Lie

Meaning: Emphatic agreements used in modern texting and digital messaging to confirm total honesty or validate someone else's statement without exaggeration.

Example: "That exam was way harder than expected, fr no cap."

Internet & Forums

TL;DR — Too Long; Didn't Read

Meaning: Standard internet shorthand placed before or after a lengthy post, providing a concise 1-2 sentence executive summary of the entire piece.

Example: "TL;DR: The project was approved and launches next Monday."

Gaming & Multiplayer

AFK & BRB — Away From Keyboard / Be Right Back

Meaning: Foundational gaming and chat terms signaling temporary absence from a match or computer, allowing teammates to coordinate accordingly.

Example: "Going afk for 2 mins to grab water, brb."

Business & Corporate

ROI & KPI — Return on Investment / Key Performance Indicator

Meaning: Core business metrics used in management consulting, marketing campaigns, and financial performance evaluations to gauge efficiency and progress.

Example: "We need to track our Q3 KPIs to ensure a healthy ROI on ad spend."
